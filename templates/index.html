About Smart Feedback Portal

Students can easily submit feedback and suggestions through a simple and user-friendly interface, ensuring their opinions are collected efficiently.

The system automatically analyzes every feedback using Artificial Intelligence and classifies it as Positive, Neutral, or Negative.

Administrators can view charts, statistics, and detailed reports to understand overall student satisfaction and identify areas that require improvement.

Feedback reports and analytics can be downloaded in PDF and CSV formats for documentation, presentations, and future reference.
